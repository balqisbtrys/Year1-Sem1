# JCorp Industrial Visit
## Summary 🌟
Date : 07 January 2026
<br> Venue : Johor Corporation, Menara KOMTAR, Johor Bahru

The visit to JCorp was attended by 32 students from TIS section 02 and accompanied by our lecturer, Dr Aryati binti Bakri. Before the session started, Encik Hazim took us on a tour around the JCorp office and workspace. The company's interior design is really giving a classy yet calming vibe.

Moving on to the sharing session, which was delivered by Encik Budiman Bujang, the Deputy Chief Digital Officer. He shared about JCorp's digital transformation journey, focusing on how AI, automation, data, and cloud technologies are used to improve efficiency and business operations. It was interesting to learn how AI supports application development, data management, and decision-making across the organization. 

Encik Yusri Mohamed also shared his insights on the importance of adaptibility and continuous learning. He highlighted that our future careers may not always align with what we study today. That is why having a strong knowledge and staying updated to technology is important for the future.

## Reflection
Throughout this visit, I gain a clearer perspective on how technology is applied beyond theoretical learning. It help me realize that computing is not about programming only, but also involve the use of technology strategically to support organizational needs and decision-making. The sharing session from Encik Budiman Bujang and Encik Yusri Mohamed, especially on adaptability and continuous learning, made me more aware of the importance of staying updated with emerging technologies such as AI and data analytics. This experience increase my interest in pursuing a technology related career and motivated me to further develop the skills that are required for the future workforce.
