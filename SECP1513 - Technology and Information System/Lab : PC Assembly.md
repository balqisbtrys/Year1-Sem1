# Lab session : PC Assembly
## Summary 🌟
Date: 30 October 2025

The PC Assembly Lab Session provided a practical session in assembling and disassembling a PC. Throughout the session, I learned how to handle each components properly such as power supply, RAM, hard disk, cooling fan, and more while following the instruction given by the staff. Their guidance as well as the teamwork from my groupmate, the PC was successfully reassembled and tested. On top of that, a quiz at the end of the session helped me in reinforcing my understanding of the computer hardware.

## Reflection
This lab session provided a valuable learning opportunity by enabling me to implement classroom knowledge in a real situation. Handling the components directly helped me understand better about their functions and placement inside the PC at once made me more confident in managing hardware. Overall, the session was very engaging as I got to explore more about computer hardware and got the opportunity to see the internal side of a computer. I’m definitely looking forward to joining more sessions like this that help me learn through real experience in the future.
